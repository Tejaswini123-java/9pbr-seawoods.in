Adani 9 PBR Seawoods

Adani 9 PBR by Adani Realty is an exclusive luxury residential development located in Nerul, Navi Mumbai, next to NRI Complex. Spread across 6.5 acres, the project features a 2-acre green podium, more than 20 modern lifestyle amenities, spacious living and dining areas, and a three-tier security system for enhanced comfort and peace of mind. Offering thoughtfully designed Elite 3 BHK , Select 3 BHK , and Luxury 3 BHK residences, the development combines contemporary architecture with premium living spaces and captivating creek and city views. The Elite 3 BHK residences offer areas ranging from 1,040 to 1,100 sq. ft., with prices starting at ₹4.7 crore onwards; the Select 3 BHK residences range from 1,300 to 1,450 sq. ft., starting at ₹5.9 crore onwards; and the Luxury 3 BHK residences range from 1,550 to 1,775 sq. ft., starting at ₹8.8 crore onwards. Exclusive Signature Penthouse ranging from 2,500 to 3,200 sq. ft. are also available on request. Strategically located for convenient access to key destinations across Navi Mumbai and Mumbai, the project offers connectivity to the Navi Mumbai International Airport, approximately 20 minutes away, and the Mumbai Trans Harbour Link (MTHL), approximately 15 minutes away, subject to traffic conditions. With its spacious residences, landscaped green spaces, modern amenities, and excellent connectivity, Adani 9 PBR offers a premium lifestyle in one of Navi Mumbai's sought-after residential locations.
